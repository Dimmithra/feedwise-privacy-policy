Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to FeedWise, a feedback management mobile application developed by Infortechgen.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information FeedWise may collect, how that information is used, and how we protect your information when you use our application.

In short: FeedWise is designed to help organizations collect and manage feedback efficiently. We use information only for providing, maintaining, securing, and improving the service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use FeedWise, we may collect information such as:

  • Account and profile information that you provide.
  • Feedback, comments, ratings, and other content submitted through the app.
  • Basic information required to identify and manage your account.
  • Device and application information necessary for security and troubleshooting.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information when required to maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through FeedWise may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the FeedWise service.
  • Collect, organize, and manage feedback.
  • Maintain and improve application functionality.
  • Provide customer and technical support.
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or technical issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Feedback and User Content

FeedWise allows users to submit feedback and other content through the application. The information contained in submitted feedback may be accessible to authorized users within the organization or account using FeedWise.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information in feedback unless it is necessary and appropriate for the intended purpose.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve FeedWise. Such providers are expected to handle information appropriately and in accordance with applicable requirements.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and safety of FeedWise, our users, or others.

5. Data Security

Infortechgen takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

FeedWise may rely on third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, authentication, notifications, or other infrastructure required to operate the application.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the FeedWise service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

FeedWise is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that information from a child has been collected improperly, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy choices.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact Infortechgen using the contact information below.
